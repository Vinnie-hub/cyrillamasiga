About the placement

Context

As part of the Maseno University Community Service Learning program, I spent time at Ratta Senior School contributing to work that sits at the intersection of environment, education, and everyday upkeep.

The program pairs university students with local schools to address practical needs on the ground — from unstable topsoil to a library that needed a system, to drainage channels doing more harm than good when left blocked. What follows is a record of that work: what was done, what it took, and what it left behind.

Duration: 8 weeks (February – April 2026) · Hours: 120+ hours of service

02

Three areas of work

Environmental Conservation

Ground work, literally

  • Planted 30+ tree species across the school compound to stabilize topsoil and reduce erosion.
  • Focused on high-vulnerability zones around the school grounds most exposed to runoff.
  • Educated students on the importance of tree cover for soil health and climate resilience.
Academic Enhancement

Making the library usable

  • Organized and categorized 5 library sections so students could actually find what they needed.
  • Ran weekly reading and mentorship sessions reaching 80+ students.
  • Built a culture of independent study and consistent library use.
Sanitation & Drainage

Keeping water moving

  • Cleared and redirected 3 blocked drainage pathways around the school compound.
  • Prevented water stagnation that could breed disease vectors like mosquitoes.
  • Maintained a cleaner, safer, and more hygienic learning environment for students and staff.

Challenges & solutions

🛠️ Tool Shortage

Initial lack of gardening tools for planting and drainage work.

→ Improvised with local resources & coordinated tool-sharing among volunteers.

📚 Low Library Turnout

Students unaware of library resources or reluctant to use them.

→ Partnered with class teachers to schedule guided library sessions and reading groups.

🌧️ Unpredictable Weather

Heavy rains disrupted outdoor planting and drainage work.

→ Adjusted timetable flexibly—focused on indoor library work during downpours.

👩‍🏫 Limited Supervision

Single intern teacher (Sirila) covering Home Science and Biology.

→ Worked independently, reported progress weekly, and collaborated closely with Sirila.
